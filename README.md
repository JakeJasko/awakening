# Awakening from the Meaning Crisis — Epistemic Companion & Interactive Quiz

A classical, contemplative interactive quiz and study companion designed to follow along with **Dr. John Vervaeke’s** landmark 50-part cognitive science and philosophical lecture series, [*Awakening from the Meaning Crisis*](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLND1JCRq8Vuh3f0P5qjrSdb5eC1ZfZwWJ).

Built with the classical editorial aesthetic, tactile micro-interactions, dual Parchment/Obsidian theming, and diagnostic rigor adapted from the [`falseidols`](https://github.com/JakeJasko/falseidols) template.

---

## Core Features

- **Episode-by-Episode Study Architecture**:
  - **Episode Primer & Thesis**: Concise, deep synthesis of Vervaeke's argument, key historical thinkers, and interactive concept tags.
  - **Epistemic Mastery Quiz**: Rich, scenario-based thought experiments designed to test genuine cognitive understanding and distinguish authentic insights from counterfeit notions.
  - **Immediate Didactic Feedback**:
    - *Why this is true to Cognitive Science*: Clear conceptual rationale rooted in Vervaeke's research.
    - *The Common Cognitive Trap / Counterfeit*: Analysis of how modern cultural baggage or self-deception leads into tempting distractors.
    - *Lecture Timestamp Reference*: Direct citation to the relevant segment in Dr. Vervaeke's lecture.
  - **Contemplative Practice & Socratic Reflection**: Socratic inquiry prompts, concrete cultivation exercises (e.g., *Sati*, *View from Above*, *Dialogos*), and a persistent personal journal.
- **Audio/Video Companion Drawer**: Listen or follow along with the YouTube lecture while taking quizzes or studying the primers.
- **Cognitive Lexicon & 4 Ways of Knowing Drawer**: Comprehensive interactive glossary defining Vervaeke's key terminology:
  - *The 4 Ways of Knowing (Propositional, Procedural, Perspectival, Participatory)*
  - *Relevance Realization & Combinatorial Explosion*
  - *Psychotechnologies & The Axial Revolution*
  - *Nomological, Normative, and Narrative Orders*
  - *Transjectivity & The Sacred*
  - *Parasitic Processing & Reciprocal Narrowing vs. Reciprocal Opening*
  - *Dialogos & The Ecology of Practices*
- **50-Episode Master Syllabus Roadmap**: Interactive guide through all 50 lectures across the 5 Epochal Arcs.
- **High-Resolution Canvas Dossier Generator**: Generates an editorial 1080x1350 (4:5 portrait) shareable completion certificate with classical Greek/Roman typography, score metrics, mastery titles (*Axial Sage*, *Dialectic Practitioner*, *Meaning Seeker*), and Socratic epigraphs.
- **Local Progress Persistence**: Tracks completed quizzes, mastery scores, study streaks, and personal reflection notes in `localStorage`.
- **Classical Scholarly Design System**:
  - Typography: *Newsreader* (editorial serif), *Cinzel* (classical Roman inscriptions), and *Plus Jakarta Sans*.
  - Dual Theme: Parchment Light Mode (`#FAF7F2`) and Contemplative Obsidian Dark Mode (`#141312`).
  - Tactile mobile thumb navigation with directional slide transitions.

## Curriculum: The 5 Epochal Arcs

| Arc | Epoch & Theme | Episodes | Core Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Arc I** | *The Axial Revolution & Classical Antiquity* | Eps 1 – 13 | The Meaning Crisis, Flow, Shamanism, Continuous Cosmos, Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, The Buddha, Insight, Stoicism |
| **Arc II** | *The Medieval Synthesis & The Fall* | Eps 14 – 20 | Gnosticism, Christianity, Agape, Aquinas, 14th-Century Nominalism (William of Ockham), The Shattered Canopy |
| **Arc III** | *The Modern Dilemma & Cognitive Alienation* | Eps 21 – 30 | Scientific Revolution, Descartes, Hobbes, Kant, Schopenhauer, Nietzsche, Nihilism, Frankfurtian Bullshit |
| **Arc IV** | *Cognitive Science & Relevance Realization* | Eps 31 – 40 | The 4 Ways of Knowing (4P/3R), Salience Landscapes, Relevance Realization, Parasitic Processing, Reciprocal Opening |
| **Arc V** | *Wisdom, Dialogos & The Ecology of Practices* | Eps 41 – 50 | Wisdom, Sophrosyne, Overcoming Foolishness, Transjectivity, Sacredness, Dialogos, The Religion That Is Not a Religion |
